Semantic diff · mathlib4

What each PR actually changed in the environment

Every declaration in mathlib is reduced to two rename-invariant hashes — one for its statement, one for its proof. Diffing two commits classifies a PR from the delta alone, not its title: what was added, generalized, reproved, renamed, or left provably untouched.

The eight coordinates

Derived axes: meaning Δ = authored_new + statement_changed · structure Δ = renamed + moved + proof_changed + repr_changed. The label is a function of the vector; where it disagrees with the PR's title prefix, a divergence flag shows both.
